What it solves

One ledger. Fresher stock. Cheaper space.

One version of truth, owned or rented

Every receipt, transfer, consumption and dispatch is written to a single registry, whichever warehouse it happened in.

  • Internal and rented warehouses side by side
  • Transfers tracked in transit, not just at each end
  • Full audit trail of who moved what, and when

Stock used before it degrades

Allocation picks the batch that expires first, automatically, for every sales order and production run.

  • FEFO batch suggested on every allocation
  • Batches flagged 30 days before they expire
  • Quarantined stock can't be allocated

Know what your space costs

Capacity and rent for every site on one dashboard, so full warehouses and idle rent show up early.

  • Space used per site, with over-capacity alerts
  • Daily rent for every rented warehouse
  • Rent-saving opportunities and capacity runway
