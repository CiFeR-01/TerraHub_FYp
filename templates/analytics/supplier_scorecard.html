How it is scored. A PO's arrival is its completion date, or failing that its latest linked inbound shipment arrival or receipt date. Its due date is the PO's expected delivery date when set, otherwise the order date plus a lead time — the supplier/material lead time on file, or the po_default_lead_time_days setting (editable in Admin › System settings). Rows scored against a derived due date are tagged EST. A PO is "on time" when arrival is on or before the due date. Fill rate is total quantity received ÷ total quantity ordered across the supplier's POs. Rating: On track ≥ 90% on-time and ≥ 98% fill; Poor below 70% on-time or below 90% fill; Watch in between. POs in Draft, Pending Approval, or Rejected are excluded. Rows load worst-first — click any column heading to re-sort.
