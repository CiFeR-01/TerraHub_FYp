How it is projected. Burn / day is total Consumed_For_Manufacturing quantity over the selected window ÷ the window length (idle days included). Days cover = available stock ÷ burn. Runs out = today + days cover. Reorder by = runs out − lead time (the largest supplier lead time on file for the material, else the po_default_lead_time_days setting, tagged EST). Critical = days cover already less than the lead time; Reorder now = reorder-by date today or past; Watch = reorder-by within 14 days. On-order quantity is shown for context but not subtracted from the projection.
