How it is projected. Burn / day is total Consumed_For_Manufacturing quantity over the selected window ÷ the window length (idle days included). Days cover = available stock ÷ burn. Runs out = today + days cover. Reorder by = runs out − lead time (the largest supplier lead time on file for the material, else the po_default_lead_time_days setting, tagged EST). 🔴 Action Required = already out of stock, or the reorder-by date is today or in the past. 🟠 Watch = reorder-by within 14 days. 🟢 Healthy = beyond that. On order (open POs) is added to the projection on each PO's expected delivery date (its own date, else order date + lead time, marked est.). A delivery that lands before the stock runs out extends days cover; one that lands after is flagged arrives after run-out because it won't prevent the stockout. Overdue POs count as arriving today.
