How it is projected. Each night snapshot_utilization records every warehouse's utilization (active-stock tonnage ÷ capacity). A least-squares line is fit through the last snapshots; its slope × 7 is the weekly rate. Days to full = (100 % − current) ÷ daily slope. Critical = at or above 95 %, or full within 14 days; Watch = full within 60 days; Filling = rising but further out; Stable = flat or emptying. Daily Rent is computed per batch: a batch with its own negotiated rate (set on the purchase order) stays at that rate for life; a batch using the warehouse's standard rate tracks that rate live, so editing a warehouse's rate immediately updates every batch that didn't negotiate its own.
